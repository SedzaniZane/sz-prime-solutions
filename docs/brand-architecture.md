# SZ Prime Solutions — Canonical Brand Architecture

Status: **FINAL / CANONICAL**

This document is the source of truth for business-unit naming across the website, CRM, database, quotations, invoices, WhatsApp flows, marketing, internal documentation and future applications.

## Parent company

### SZ Prime Solutions (Pty) Ltd
The legal entity, master brand and central operating company.

Its responsibilities include:
- strategy and governance
- finance and revenue oversight
- customer data and CRM
- technology and automation
- shared marketing
- partner standards
- quality control
- cross-unit customer journeys
- expansion and productisation

## Final customer-facing units

### SZ Prime Living Accommodations
Accommodation discovery, rentals, landlord connections and living support.

### SZ Prime Relocations
Moving, transport coordination, relocation support and move-in setup.

### SZ Pristine Cleaning
Residential, commercial, turnover, move-in and move-out cleaning.

### SZ Prime Plug-Connect
Trusted service-provider discovery, matching, brokerage and coordination.

### SZ Prime Facilities
Facilities management, property operations and recurring B2B service coordination.

### SZ Prime Tech Hub
Technology, websites, software, AI, automations, dashboards, chatbots and internal systems.

### SZ Prime Business Solutions
SME support, consulting, business enablement, operational support and packaged business solutions.

## Naming principle

SZ Prime Solutions is the umbrella promise. Most units carry the **SZ Prime** family name so customer trust compounds into one master brand.

**SZ Pristine Cleaning** intentionally preserves the Pristine identity because it directly signals the cleaning proposition while remaining visibly part of the SZ family.
