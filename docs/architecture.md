# SZ Prime Solutions Platform Architecture

## Core principle
One customer relationship, multiple specialised service pathways.

## Experience layer
- WhatsApp Business
- Public website
- Future customer portal
- Future partner portal

## Intelligence and routing layer
- Customer intent classification
- Language detection and multilingual replies
- Business-unit routing
- Human escalation
- Future specialised agents

## Operations layer
- CRM / customer records
- Service requests
- Quotations
- Bookings
- Payments
- Partner assignments
- Service delivery
- Feedback and cross-sell

## Business units
- SZ Pristine Solutions
- ZaNdou Living
- ZaNdou Relocations
- ZaNdou Connect
- SZ Prime Facilities
- SZ Business Solutions
- SZ Tech Hub

## Planned technology
- Next.js + TypeScript
- Supabase / PostgreSQL
- WhatsApp Business Platform
- Make or n8n
- Yoco
- AI provider/API
- GitHub as source control

## Productisation model
1. Solve an internal SZ Prime problem.
2. Run the solution operationally.
3. Measure reliability and business value.
4. Standardise it.
5. Package it through SZ Tech Hub for external clients.

## Safety and governance
Human approval remains required for financial commitments, tender submissions, sensitive customer decisions and high-risk automated actions.
