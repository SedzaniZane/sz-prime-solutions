# SZ Prime Solutions Platform Architecture

## Core principle
One customer relationship, multiple specialised service pathways under one SZ Prime master brand.

## Experience layer
- WhatsApp Business
- Public website
- Future customer portal
- Future partner portal

## Intelligence and routing layer
- Customer intent classification
- Language detection and multilingual replies
- Business-unit routing
- Human escalation
- Future specialised agents

## Operations layer
- CRM / customer records
- Service requests
- Quotations
- Bookings
- Payments
- Partner assignments
- Service delivery
- Feedback and cross-sell

## Canonical business units
- SZ Prime Living Accommodations
- SZ Prime Relocations
- SZ Pristine Cleaning
- SZ Prime Plug-Connect
- SZ Prime Facilities
- SZ Prime Tech Hub
- SZ Prime Business Solutions

## Master brand
**SZ Prime Solutions (Pty) Ltd** owns and coordinates the operating system, customer data, governance, technology, finance and cross-unit customer journey.

## Planned technology
- Next.js + TypeScript
- Supabase / PostgreSQL
- Vercel
- WhatsApp Business Platform
- Make or n8n
- Yoco
- AI provider/API
- GitHub as source control

## Productisation model
1. Solve an internal SZ Prime problem.
2. Run the solution operationally.
3. Measure reliability and business value.
4. Standardise it.
5. Package it through SZ Prime Tech Hub for external clients.

## Safety and governance
Human approval remains required for financial commitments, tender submissions, sensitive customer decisions and high-risk automated actions.
